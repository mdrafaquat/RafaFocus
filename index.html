RafaFocus FAQ

Quick answers about the Learning Player and study tools.

What is RafaFocus?

RafaFocus is a lightweight student productivity website centered on a YouTube Learning Player, focus timer, digital study clock, tasks and study statistics.

Does RafaFocus require an account?

No. The core study tools work directly in the browser, and personal task and focus data can be stored locally on the device.

Which YouTube links can I use?

The Learning Player supports standard YouTube videos, Shorts, Live links, youtu.be links and YouTube embed links when the video allows embedding.

Is RafaFocus free?

RafaFocus is designed as a free, lightweight study workspace with no required paid subscription for its core tools.

The idea behind RafaFocus

The idea is simple: make it easier for students to focus on what they are learning instead of getting distracted by everything around them. RafaFocus brings useful study utilities together in one clean workspace while keeping the experience lightweight and easy to understand.

RafaFocus is designed with simplicity in mind—easy to use, organized around the things that matter during a study session, and flexible enough to fit different learning routines.
